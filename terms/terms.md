## 1. Description of the Service

TCG Companion is an application designed to assist trading card game players and collectors in organizing, managing, cataloging, and scanning their physical card collections and wishlists.

The App features a modular card game architecture, launching with initial support for the **Pokémon Trading Card Game**. The App operates primarily through local on-device storage, with optional cloud backup and synchronization via the user's personal Google Drive account.
