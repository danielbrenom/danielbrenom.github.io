TCG Companion

Collection Management, Scanner & Wishlist Companion
Initial Support: Pokémon TCG

TCG Companion is a modern, privacy-focused collection tracker designed to simplify organizing, searching, and managing your trading card library. Engineered with a modular card game architecture, the app launches with deep, comprehensive support for the Pokémon Trading Card Game, with future expansion planned for additional popular TCGs.

Local-First Storage
Your cards, variants, quantities, and wishlists stay securely stored on your device database without mandatory account sign-up.
On-Device OCR Scanner
Scan physical cards using real-time camera computer vision (OpenCV & Tesseract OCR). Image processing occurs entirely on your device.
Optional Google Drive Sync
Seamlessly back up and synchronize collections across your Android devices directly via your own private Google Drive AppData folder.
Modular TCG Architecture
Built to scale across different trading card ecosystems while maintaining dedicated set databases, card rarities, and variant tracking.
