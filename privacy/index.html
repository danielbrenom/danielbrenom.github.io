Guiomarino Dev & Game ("Guiomarino", "we", "us", or "our") develops and maintains TCG Companion (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, where that information is stored, when information is transmitted to third-party services, and the choices available to users.

Summary of Core Principles

TCG Companion is built local-first. Your card collections, wishlists, and scanner operations remain on your device. We do not operate user accounts or remote database servers to track your inventory. Optional cloud sync runs via your private Google Drive space.

1. About TCG Companion

TCG Companion is an application designed for managing, searching, and tracking Trading Card Game (TCG) collections. Built with an extensible modular multi-TCG architecture, the App launches with comprehensive initial support for the Pokémon Trading Card Game.

The App is designed to operate primarily with local storage on the user's device. Optional features, such as cloud synchronization through Google Drive and advertising, involve third-party services.

2. Information stored locally on your device

Most information used by the App remains exclusively on the user's device. The App may store locally:

  • card identifiers and set data;
  • card variants, such as Normal, Reverse Holo, and Holo;
  • card quantities and acquired counts;
  • card condition/quality ratings;
  • wishlist items and wishlist dates;
  • theme and visual preferences;
  • synchronization settings and state;
  • last synchronization timestamp;
  • an app-generated random device GUID used to resolve synchronization conflicts;
  • advertising-related counters and local reward state;
  • cache and update settings.

The App does not transmit these locally stored values to servers operated by Guiomarino Dev & Game.

3. Google Drive synchronization

The App provides an optional synchronization feature using Google Drive.

When enabled by the user, the App uploads the data required to synchronize the collection and wishlist to the user's own Google account. The App requests only the Google Drive drive.appdata scope, and synchronization data is stored in Google's private appDataFolder.

Synchronized data includes collection data, wishlist data, synchronization metadata, an app-generated random device GUID, timestamps, and revision information.

The App does not use synchronized collection data for advertising, profiling, sale of information, or unrelated purposes, and does not maintain its own server-side copy of the user's collection.

The synchronization metadata contains SchemaVersion, DeviceId, LastModified, and Revision. The DeviceId is a random GUID generated by the App, not a hardware identifier such as an IMEI or serial number. It is used only to resolve conflicts between devices and is included in sync.json stored in the user's private Google Drive application-data area.

4. Google authentication

To enable Google Drive synchronization, the App uses Google's authorization mechanisms and requests only the drive.appdata authorization scope.

The App receives a short-lived OAuth access token required for authorized Google Drive API requests. It does not request or receive the user's Google email address, name, profile picture, or Google account ID.

The OAuth access token is stored locally using the device's secure storage facilities. The App does not receive or store the user's Google account password.

5. Google AdMob and advertising

TCG Companion uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Depending on location, consent choices, and applicable requirements, the advertising SDK may process:

  • Google Advertising ID;
  • device identifiers and characteristics, such as model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen characteristics, and carrier information;
  • IP address;
  • approximate geographic location derived from IP address;
  • advertisement impressions and interactions, including clicks and rewarded-ad interactions;
  • technical advertising SDK diagnostics, such as request latency, fill information, and advertising-related failures.

The App does not request precise device location and does not request GPS permissions.

Personalized advertisements may be shown where permitted and where required consent has been obtained. Non-personalized advertisements may be shown when personalized advertising consent is not available or has been refused.

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising consent. Users can review or change advertising privacy preferences through Settings → Manage Ad Privacy Options.

The App does not directly sell users' personal information to advertisers. Data processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's own privacy and data-retention practices.

6. Card and game data from external services

To provide card catalogs, images, and card details, the App interfaces with third-party data providers depending on the card game:

Pokémon TCG Module: TCGdex & PokeAPI

  • TCGdex: The App uses the public TCGdex API to retrieve Pokémon Trading Card Game card information, including names, descriptions, metadata, and card artwork. Card artwork and metadata may be cached locally on device for performance. The App does not send the user's collection or wishlist to TCGdex. When the card scanner recognizes a card name, the App may send the recognized card name as a text search query to TCGdex to retrieve card details.
  • PokeAPI: The App uses public Pokémon sprite and reference resources provided through the PokeAPI ecosystem. Sprites may be cached locally. User collection or personal data is never transmitted to PokeAPI.

7. Camera and card scanning

The App includes a card scanning feature that uses the device camera to assist in identifying physical trading cards. Camera frames are processed entirely on the device using OpenCV and Tesseract OCR with a locally bundled language model.

The App does not send camera photos, video frames, image crops, or raw OCR text to Guiomarino Dev & Game, card data providers (e.g. TCGdex), Google, AdMob, or other remote services.

After local recognition occurs on-device, the recognized card name text may be sent as a search query to public card APIs (such as TCGdex for Pokémon TCG) to retrieve matching public card metadata.

8. Network communications

The App may communicate over HTTPS with:

  • Card Data APIs (e.g., TCGdex API & CDN, PokeAPI) — public card information, artwork, and reference sprites;
  • Google Drive API / Google Identity — optional collection synchronization and authorization;
  • Google AdMob / Google UMP — advertising display and consent management.

The App does not operate a proprietary backend server that receives or stores the user's collection.

9. Data sharing

Guiomarino Dev & Game does not sell users' personal information.

Depending on features used, information may be processed by:

  • Google: authentication and optional Google Drive synchronization;
  • Google AdMob: advertising, advertising measurement, fraud prevention, and related technical diagnostics;
  • TCGdex & PokeAPI: public card metadata, card artwork, and sprite assets for the Pokémon TCG module.

The user's collection, wishlist, and synchronization metadata are transmitted to Google Drive only when synchronization is enabled. The App does not send the user's collection or wishlist to card data providers.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical measures designed to protect information processed by the App, including local storage of collection data, secure local storage for OAuth credentials, HTTPS network communications, Google's authorization mechanisms, and minimization of information transmitted outside the device.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

11. Data retention and deletion

Local data

Data stored locally remains on the device until deleted by the user, the App, or the operating system. Uninstalling the Android App removes its local database, cache, secure-storage entries, and application preferences from the device.

Google Drive data

Cloud synchronization data remains in the user's Google Drive application-data area until deleted.

The App provides:

  • Delete My Collection & Wishlist
  • Disconnect Google Account → Delete Backup

If the user chooses to keep the backup while disconnecting Google, the cloud backup remains in Google Drive and only local authentication credentials are removed.

If the App is uninstalled, cloud synchronization data cannot automatically be deleted by the uninstaller because the App is no longer present to execute the deletion request. The user can remove cloud data by reinstalling the App and using its deletion controls or through Google Drive's application data management settings.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights including confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion, restriction where applicable, portability, information about data sharing, withdrawal of consent where applicable, and objection to certain processing activities.

For requests concerning processing performed by Guiomarino Dev & Game, contact us using the contact information below. Where information is processed directly by a third-party provider (such as Google AdMob or Google Drive), requests may need to be directed to that provider.

13. Third-party services and policies

The App uses Google / Google Identity / Google Drive, Google AdMob / Google User Messaging Platform, TCGdex, and PokeAPI. Users should review the privacy practices of these providers to understand how they process information under their own responsibility:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Drive Terms of Service
  • TCGdex Website
  • PokeAPI Website

14. Children's privacy

TCG Companion is not designed to request or intentionally collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating user profiles. The App does not intentionally request a child's name, email address, or other profile information.

If a parent or legal guardian believes that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us using the contact information below.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its supported card games, third-party integrations, or applicable legal requirements change. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.
