🃏 Supported Card Games & Roadmap

Which trading card games are currently supported in TCG Companion? ▼

TCG Companion is built from the ground up with an extensible, modular card game architecture. The app launches with deep, comprehensive initial support for the Pokémon Trading Card Game, featuring complete card databases, variant tracking (Normal, Holo, Reverse Holo), card condition ratings, and price/image lookups.

Support for additional popular trading card games is actively in planning and development.

How can I suggest support for a new card game or set? ▼

We love community input! If there is a specific card game or expansion set you would like to see supported next, please send an email to guiomarinodev@gmail.com with the subject line [TCG Feature Request] Card Game Name.

☁️ Google Drive Backup & Synchronization

How does Google Drive synchronization work? ▼

When you connect your Google Account in the app settings, TCG Companion uses the restricted drive.appdata scope to store your collection and wishlist in your own Google Drive application data folder (appDataFolder).

  • Your collection file is private to your Google account and hidden from regular Drive folders.
  • Guiomarino Dev & Game does not store your inventory on external servers.
  • Device IDs used to resolve sync revisions are randomly generated GUIDs, not hardware identifiers.
What should I do if sync fails or reports an authorization error? ▼

If you encounter synchronization errors:

  1. Verify your device has a stable internet connection.
  2. Open Settings → Google Drive Sync in the app.
  3. Tap Disconnect Google Account, then tap Connect Google Account to refresh your OAuth authorization token.
  4. Tap Sync Now to initiate a manual backup.
What happens to my data if I switch devices or uninstall the app? ▼

Uninstalling the app: Deletes local database files and preferences from the device. However, if you had Google Drive Sync enabled, your backup remains safe in your Google Drive.

Restoring on a new device: Install TCG Companion, navigate to Settings → Google Drive Sync, connect the same Google account, and select Restore from Google Drive.

📷 Camera & OCR Card Scanner Tips

How can I improve card scanner accuracy? ▼

Follow these quick tips for optimal scanning speed and accuracy:

  • Lighting: Scan in a well-lit room with indirect lighting to prevent harsh glare or reflections on card sleeves.
  • Background: Place the card on a plain, contrasting background (e.g., a dark playmat for light cards, or a light surface for dark cards).
  • Alignment: Align the card name banner within the on-screen scanner guide frame.
  • Camera Focus: Hold your device steady 15–20 cm above the card until the autofocus locks on the card title text.
Is my camera video or image sent to remote servers during scanning? ▼

No. Image processing is handled 100% locally on your device using OpenCV and Tesseract OCR with an embedded on-device model. No camera frames, video clips, or photos are ever uploaded or transmitted to any remote server.
