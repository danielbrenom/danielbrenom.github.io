🛡️ Pokémon TCG Companion — Data Deletion

✉️

Instructions

Pokémon TCG Companion does not create user accounts.

Users can delete locally stored collection and wishlist data directly from the application.

Users who have enabled Google Drive synchronization can delete their cloud synchronization backup using Settings → Google Drive → Delete My Collection & Wishlist or Disconnect Google Account → Delete Backup.

If you need assistance requesting deletion of data associated with Pokémon TCG Companion, contact:
